1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================
Job searching in technical disciplines is plagued by severe friction:
  - Candidates submit generic, non-optimized resumes that get filtered out by 
    Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) with 75%+ rejection rates before human review.
  - Job opportunities are fragmented across startup job boards (Internshala, 
    CutShort, Instahyre, Wellfound), aggregator boards (Naukri, Foundit), and 
    hundreds of isolated enterprise corporate career portals.
  - Applying is tedious, repetitive, and time-consuming, while cold email outreach 
    is often done poorly with mass generic BCC blasts that hit spam filters.
  - Candidates lack real-time feedback on why their applications are stalling, 
    and lack company-specific interview preparation once an interview is scheduled.

NextOpportunityFind solves this holistically by deploying a swarm of 9 
autonomous AI agents that execute the entire job search pipeline:
  1. Ingests candidate resumes, benchmarks against ATS algorithms, and provides 
     an interactive drag-and-drop resume editor.
  2. Aggregates thousands of verified startup and MNC roles across India and 
     remote-global markets.
  3. Matches candidates semantically using multi-criteria weighted algorithms.
  4. Rewrites candidate resumes using high-selection-rate pattern templates 
     (quantifying bullet achievements without fabricating facts).
  5. Routes applications through Playwright ATS auto-fill or staged cold outreach.
  6. Tracks applications through an interactive Kanban board while diagnosing 
     rejection trends.
  7. Prepares candidates with live AI mock interviews, company dossiers, coding 
     practice, and curated video masterclasses.

3.1 AGENT 1: DEEP RESUME PARSER & 5-PILLAR ATS EVALUATOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Primary File: `backend/app/agents/agent1_parser.py`
- Ingestion Engine:
    * PDF Parsing: `pdfplumber` for precise bounding-box layout extraction 
      and table preservation, with PyPDF2 text fallback.
    * DOCX Parsing: `python-docx` extracting paragraph hierarchy, bold bullet 
      runs, and table cell contents.
- Extracted Entities:
    * Full Name, Email, Phone, Location (City, Country, Remote willingness).
    * Technical Skills list (languages, frameworks, databases, cloud, DevOps).
    * Domain Specializations (FinTech, EdTech, AI/ML, SaaS, E-Commerce, etc.).
    * Years of Experience & Detailed Work History (Role, Company, Dates, Bullets).
    * Education History (Degree, Field, University, Graduation Year, GPA).
    * Projects Portfolio (Title, Description, Technologies used, GitHub links).
- 5-Pillar ATS Benchmark Scoring Formula (0 - 100 Points):
    1. Skills Density & Contextual Relevance (35 Points):
       Evaluates whether skills are substantiated within project and experience 
       descriptions rather than listed as empty keyword stuffing.
    2. Action Verbs & Metric Quantification (25 Points):
       Detects high-impact action verbs (Architected, Spearheaded, Accelerated, 
       Engineered, Reduced, Scaled) paired with measurable quantities 
       (%, $, ms, Xk users, TPS).
    3. Contact Information Completeness (15 Points):
       Verifies presence of valid professional email, phone number, location, 
       LinkedIn URL, and GitHub/Portfolio URL.
    4. Layout, Formatting & Single-Column Readability (15 Points):
       Audits resume structure to ensure standard headers (Summary, Skills, 
       Experience, Projects, Education) and flags multi-column parsing hazards.
    5. Keyword Clustering & Seniority Tone (10 Points):
       Measures balance between foundational fundamentals and advanced tools.
- Dual-State Resume Architecture:
    * `raw_extracted_content`: Immutable parsed snapshot from original upload.
    * `working_content`: Live, editable state updated via the web studio.
    * `section_order`: Configurable list determining render sequence.

3.4 AGENT 3: MULTI-CRITERIA SEMANTIC MATCHING ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Primary File: `backend/app/agents/agent3_matching.py`
- Multi-Dimensional Weighted Compatibility Formula:
    Match Score = (0.40 * Skill Overlap) + 
                  (0.25 * Domain Relevance) + 
                  (0.15 * Location Compatibility) + 
                  (0.20 * Semantic Title/Experience Score)
- Component Calculation Breakdown:
    * Skill Overlap (40%): Calculates Jaccard similarity and exact keyword 
      intersection between candidate skills and job requirements.
    * Domain Relevance (25%): Matches candidate background domains against 
      the target job domain.
    * Location Compatibility (15%): Returns 100% for remote matches; 100% for 
      city matches; 75% for preferred relocation regions.
    * Semantic Match (20%): Evaluates experience level and title alignment.
- Fresher / Internship Dynamic Adjustment:
    * For internships, experience penalties are zeroed out, shifting weight 
      to project portfolio relevance and core programming fundamentals.
- Qualification Gate:
    * Roles with Match Score >= 65.0% are classified as "Qualified" and 
      automatically generate a record in `matches` and `applications`.

3.5 AGENT 4: RESUME PROFESSIONAL AGENT & PATTERN REWRITER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Primary Files: `backend/app/agents/agent4_resume_professional.py`, 
                 `backend/app/agents/agent4_tailor.py`
- Pattern-Based Template Architectures:
    1. MNC Reverse-Chronological Standard: Clean single-column layout, 
       strict bullet hierarchy, standard font sizing.
    2. Startup Impact Pattern: High-emphasis on metrics, speed of execution, 
       and end-to-end project ownership.
    3. Executive Technical Lead Pattern: Highlights architectural design, 
       mentorship, and business impact.
- Anti-Hallucination Bullet Transformation Formula:
    [Strong Action Verb] + [Specific Task / Technical Context] + [Quantifiable Impact Metric]
    * Example Before: "Helped with payment system and made it faster."
    * Example After:  "Architected asynchronous payment processing worker in FastAPI, 
                       reducing checkout latency by 42% across 50,000 daily transactions."
- Recomputed ATS Validation:
    * Evaluates post-tailoring ATS score against pre-tailoring score.
    * Stores full diff audit (`summary_changes`, `bullet_edits`, `skill_reordering`) 
      in `resumes_tailored`.

3.7 AGENT 5: SMART ATS APPLICATION ROUTER & AUTO-FILL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Primary Files: `backend/app/main.py`, `nextopportunityfind/routing/`
- Application Routing Matrix:
    * Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby: Direct auto-apply eligible via Playwright.
    * Company Career Portals: Form auto-fill engine extracts fields; forms 
      with low confidence scores are routed to Manual Review.
    * Native Boards (LinkedIn, Naukri, Internshala): Enforced as Discovery-Only 
      to protect candidate accounts and respect terms of service.
- Dry-Run Safety Mode:
    * Auto-apply defaults to `dry_run=True`, staging the filled application 
      for candidate visual inspection and approval before submission.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.8 AGENT 6: THROTTLED 1:1 BATCH COLD EMAIL OUTREACH ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Primary File: `backend/app/agents/agent6_batch_email.py`
- Cold Outreach Logic:
    * Specifically targets startup listings requesting email applications 
      (`careers@company.com`).
    * Generates 1:1 hyper-personalized email subject lines and body copy 
      citing specific company projects and candidate technical fits.
    * Attaches the job-tailored PDF resume generated by Agent 4.
- Safety & Anti-Spam Controls:
    * Staged Review Queue: All drafts must be approved by the candidate.
    * Rate Throttling: Configurable sending rate (e.g. max 20 emails/hour).
    * Per-Company Cooldown: Prevents sending multiple emails to the same firm.
    * Global Do-Not-Contact Blacklist: Honors opt-outs and restricted domains.
    * Full Logging: Tracks message IDs, timestamps, and bounce status in `email_log`.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.9 AGENT 7: OUTCOME INTELLIGENCE & APPLICATION KANBAN LIFECYCLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Primary Files: `backend/app/agents/agent7_outcome_intelligence.py`, 
                 `backend/app/agents/outcome_tracker.py`
- Lifecycle Stages:
    `matched` ──▶ `tailored` ──▶ `pending_manual_review` ──▶ `submitted` / `emailed`
        │
        ├──▶ `interview_scheduled` ──▶ `offer_received` ──▶ `hired`
        └──▶ `rejected`
- Automated AI Outcome Diagnostics:
    * Rejection at Screening: Detects missing required keywords or low ATS density.
    * Domain Misalignment: Detects gaps in required stack (e.g., Redis, Kafka, K8s).
    * Actionable Recommendations: Generates real-time suggestions on profile enhancements.

5. COMPLETE REST API ENDPOINTS SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

[PROFILE & RESUME ANALYZER]
  - `POST /api/profile/upload`
      Request: `multipart/form-data` with `file: UploadFile` (.pdf or .docx)
      Action: Ingests resume, runs Agent 1 parser & ATS evaluation, triggers discovery sweep.
      Response: Full Profile schema with ATS breakdown.
  - `GET  /api/profile`
      Response: Current Profile JSON with working content, skills, experience, ATS score.
  - `POST /api/profile`
      Request: ProfileSchema JSON payload (manual update / working state save).
      Response: Updated Profile JSON.
  - `POST /api/profile/reorder`
      Request: `{"section_order": ["summary", "experience", "skills", "projects", "education"]}`
      Response: Updated section order confirmation.
  - `GET  /api/profile/export/pdf`
      Response: Binary `application/pdf` streamed download of high-ATS resume.
  - `GET  /api/profile/export/docx`
      Response: Binary `application/vnd.openxmlformats...` Word document download.
  - `GET  /api/profile/export/md`
      Response: Plain text `text/plain` Markdown export.

[JOB DISCOVERY & MNC SCANNER]
  - `GET  /api/jobs`
      Query Parameters: `domain`, `source`, `location`, `role_type`, `remote`, `source_category`, `company_tier`
      Response: Array of JobSchema records.
  - `POST /api/jobs/discover`
      Action: Triggers live discovery sweep across Indian startup boards.
      Response: `{"status": "success", "jobs_discovered": 85}`
  - `POST /api/jobs/mnc-scan`
      Action: Triggers Agent 2b enterprise career portal scanner.
      Response: `{"status": "success", "scanned_enterprises": 12, "new_roles": 45}`
  - `GET  /api/jobs/mnc-scan/status`
      Response: Latest scan audit logs from `mnc_scan_log`.

[MATCHING & RESUME TAILORING]
  - `GET  /api/matches`
      Response: Array of MatchSchema records with 4-component breakdowns & skill gaps.
  - `POST /api/resume/tailor/{match_id}`
      Action: Runs Agent 4 pattern-based rewriting against target job.
      Response: TailoredResumeSchema with diff summary and before/after ATS scores.
  - `GET  /api/resume/templates`
      Response: Array of curated ResumeTemplateSchema definitions.

[APPLICATION PIPELINE & OUTREACH]

7. SAFETY, COMPLIANCE & ETHICAL POLICIES
================================================================================
1. Dry-Run Default for Auto-Apply:
   - Automated form-filling through Playwright runs in `dry_run=True` mode by default, 
     requiring candidate confirmation before final submission.
2. Discovery-Only Policy for Protected Portals:
   - LinkedIn, Naukri, and Internshala are utilized strictly as discovery feeds. 
     No automated login or Easy Apply scripts are permitted, strictly upholding terms of service.
3. Anti-Spam Email Outreach Safeguards:
   - 1:1 personalized outreach to email-first listings.
   - Throttled sending rate (max 20 emails/hour).
   - Candidate review queue before sending.
   - Cooldown periods per company and global Do-Not-Contact blacklist.
4. Zero-Hallucination Resume Policy:
   - Agent 4 strictly restructures and improves the phrasing of candidate-provided 
     facts. It never fabricates employers, degrees, dates, or false metrics.
