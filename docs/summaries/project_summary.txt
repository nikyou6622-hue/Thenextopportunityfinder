1. EXECUTIVE IDENTITY & CORE MISSION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Thenextopportunity is an end-to-end, 100% free, privacy-first AI Career Acceleration Operating System 
engineered for software engineers, tech students (B.Tech/BE/MCA/BCA), freshers, and career switchers 
in India and globally.

The platform eliminates the primary frictions of modern job seeking:
  1. ATS Black Box: Resumes fail ATS screens due to keyword mismatches, broken tables, or unquantified 
     impact. The platform provides a 5-pillar mathematical score (0-100) and 11 certified templates.
  2. Fake & Expired Postings: Aggregators spam users with dead links and affiliate redirects. 
     The platform revalidates direct canonical ATS portals (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday).
  3. Hallucinatory AI Tools: Generic LLMs invent unearned degrees, companies, and metrics. 
     The platform's zero-hallucination engine is strictly constrained to candidate-verified facts.
  4. Fragmented Tooling: Consolidates job discovery, ATS resume editing, 1-click tailoring, 
     Kanban tracking, voice mock interviews, coding sandboxes, and cold email outreach into one app.
  5. Privacy Exploitation: Implements India DPDP Act (2023) standards with field-level AES-256 Fernet 
     encryption, 1-click hard cascade purge (Right to Erasure), and zero third-party data selling.

3. MULTI-AGENT ARCHITECTURE & SYSTEM ROLES
====================================================================================================
The backend is structured into 11 autonomous agents, scrapers, and routers (`backend/app/agents/`):

1. Agent 1: Resume Parser & Canonical ATS Scorer (`agent1_parser.py` - ~850 lines)
   - Parses `.pdf` (via `pdfplumber`), `.docx` (via `python-docx`), and raw `.txt` files.
   - Extracts contact details, 1,500+ tech skills, work experience, projects, and education.
   - Computes mathematical 5-pillar ATS score (0–100).
   - Encrypts raw candidate text with AES-256 Fernet before persisting to `ProfileModel`.

2. Agent 2: Opportunity Ingestion Engine (`agent2_discovery.py`)
   - Ingests structured opportunities from local databases and verified RSS feeds.
   - Normalizes locations (Remote/Hybrid/Onsite), experience levels, and role categories.

3. Agent 2B: Big-MNC Career Portal Scanner (`agent2b_mnc_scanner.py` - ~1,100 lines)
   - Direct career portal scraper for Tier-1 Global Tech & Indian IT Giants:
     Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Apple, Meta, TCS (iBegin/NQT), Infosys (Springboard), Wipro, Accenture.
   - Adheres to robots.txt, rotates user agents, and filters for live, direct apply URLs.

4. Agent 2C: India Internships & Campus Scraper (`agent2c_india_internships_scraper.py` - ~1,000 lines)
   - Aggregates internships from Unstop, Cuvette, Internshala, Wellfound, and GitHub Campus Repos.
   - Normalizes INR monthly stipends (e.g. ₹25,000/month), detects PPO conversion opportunities.

5. Agent 2D: Global Tech Jobs Scraper (`agent2d_global_jobs_scraper.py`)
   - Scrapes FreeHire 50-ATS aggregator and LinkedIn public guest job feed.

6. Agent 3: Multi-Factor Semantic Matchmaker (`agent3_matching.py`)
   - Calculates weighted candidate-to-job compatibility score:
     Match Score = 0.40*(Skill Overlap) + 0.25*(Semantic Cosine) + 0.20*(Domain) + 0.15*(Location).
   - Identifies matching skills and missing skill gaps.

7. Agent 4: Zero-Hallucination Tailor & Multi-Format Exporter (`agent4_tailor.py` & `agent4_export_generator.py`)
   - Rewrites candidate resumes targeted to a specific job description with zero invented facts.
   - Generates production-ready exports: ReportLab PDF, Python-Docx Word, LaTeX Jinja, and Markdown.

8. Agent 5: System Reporting & Analytics Engine (`agent5_reporting.py`)
   - Calculates platform-wide telemetry, ATS score distribution curves, and hiring pipeline conversion.

9. Agent 6: Recruiter Cold Outreach Studio (`agent6_batch_email.py`)
   - Generates personalized recruiter cold emails (Formal, Casual, Referral Request).
   - Prepares batch dispatch queues and simulates SMTP delivery.

10. Agent 7: Outcome Intelligence & Bottleneck Diagnosis (`agent7_outcome_intelligence.py`)
    - Tracks longitudinal application progression; diagnoses screening drop-offs and resume bottlenecks.

11. Agent 8: AI Mock Interview Coach & Question Bank Studio (`agent8_interview_prep.py` - ~1,600 lines)
    - Generates company briefing dossiers (tech stack, funding stage, engineering architecture, news).
    - Generates 10 customized questions (5 technical + 5 STAR behavioral) per job.
    - Evaluates candidate audio/text responses across 4 rubric dimensions (1–100).
    - Accesses 1,000+ question bank and recommends curated learning resources.

12. Source Router: Direct ATS Link Hardener & Revalidator (`source_router.py`)
    - Strips affiliate/UTM parameters and resolves tracking links to canonical ATS portals (Greenhouse, 
      Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters). Performs automated HTTP HEAD/GET health revalidations.

7. ATS 5-PILLAR RUBRIC & 11 CERTIFIED TEMPLATES
====================================================================================================
Mathematical 5-Pillar Score Formula:
  ATS Score = Skills (35) + Impact/Metrics (25) + Contact Info (15) + Structure/AST (15) + Domain Keywords (10)

Score Breakdown Rubric:
  - Skills Coverage (35 pts): Keyword density of technical languages, frameworks, databases, tools.
  - Impact Metrics (25 pts) : Quantified action verbs (e.g., "Decreased latency by 35%", "Scaled to 100k DAU").
  - Contact & Links (15 pts): Verified email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio link validity.
  - Structural AST (15 pts) : Section hierarchy (`summary`, `skills`, `experience`, `projects`, `education`).
  - Domain Keywords (10 pts): Alignment with target role category (Full Stack, Backend, AI/ML, DevOps).

11 Certified Layout Templates (`web/src/utils/resumeTemplates.js`):
  1. Harvard Classic (ATS Safe)       : Standard single-column, serif headings, strict top-to-bottom AST.
  2. Modern Clean (Default)          : Minimalist sans-serif, high readability, compact margins.
  3. Executive Minimalist            : Elegant corporate structure for senior leadership roles.
  4. Silicon Valley Compact          : Ultra-dense single-page format for high-experience engineers.
  5. Left Rail Tech                  : Sidebar layout with skills/tools on left, experience on right.
  6. Academic / Research             : Multi-page layout emphasizing publications and research.
  7. Two-Column Modern               : Balanced dual-column design for mid-level developers.
  8. Creative Portfolio              : Color accents and visual portfolio highlights for designers/frontend.
  9. Latex ModernCV (Engineering)    : Jinja2 LaTeX engine rendering academic/engineering CVs.
  10. Latex Deedy Style              : High-density two-column LaTeX layout favored by CS graduates.
  11. Latex Sleek Indigo             : Contemporary accented LaTeX template with modern typography.

8. SECURITY, GUARDRAILS & INDIA DPDP ACT (2023) COMPLIANCE
====================================================================================================
1. Prompt Injection Sandboxing (`llm_guardrails.py`):
   - Sanitizes angle brackets (`<` -> `&lt;`, `>` -> `&gt;`).
   - Wraps candidate data inside XML container blocks: `<candidate_resume_text>` and `<job_description_text>`.
   - Directs the LLM to treat XML contents purely as inert raw data, neutralizing adversarial instructions.

2. Zero-Hallucination Standard:
   - Constrained strictly to candidate-verified facts. The LLM is prohibited from inventing fake companies,
     GPA, graduation dates, or metrics.
   - Pydantic validation (`TailoredResumeSchema`) enforces strict schema output with up to 2 retry loops.

3. India DPDP Act (2023) Architecture:
   - Lawful Consent Capture (Section 6) : Explicit checkbox recorded with timestamp in `ProfileModel`.
   - Field-Level Encryption (Section 8) : AES-256 Fernet encryption for `raw_resume_text` before storage.
   - Right to Erasure (Section 12)      : Hard cascade purge (`POST /api/profile/reset`) deletes records 
                                          atomically across all 19 database tables.
   - Data Portability (Section 13)      : 1-click JSON export of complete candidate profile.
   - Retention Minimization (Section 9) : Automated 90-day purge of logs and transient data.

4. Secrets & Rate Limiting:
   - 0 hardcoded API keys in source code (loaded via `.env`).
   - Leaky-bucket rate limiter per IP/profile (60 requests/min).
   - Real-time token and USD cost tracking logged in `llm_usage_logs`.
